# What Fithara Does

Fithara is an assistant that helps an author pick and draft a public license for a written work — the layer that decides what readers and other people are allowed to do with it (share it, adapt it, sell it, and so on).

It does this by:

- Offering a curated set of well-known licenses (like the Creative Commons family) to choose from.
- Talking through what the author wants, in plain language, and recommending a license that fits.
- Producing a draft: either one of those licenses word-for-word, a modified version of one, or something fully custom if nothing standard fits.

Each reply the assistant gives is a standalone answer to that one message — it doesn't remember anything from previous conversations on its own. Whatever app is showing this to the author keeps track of the conversation and the current draft, and sends it back to Fithara each time.

## What Fithara Does Not Do

- It is not a legal advice service. Every draft comes with a reminder to have it reviewed by a real lawyer before it's used.
- It does not store anything. No accounts, no saved drafts, no history — if the surrounding app loses the conversation, Fithara has no way to recover it.
- It does not decide who owns a work, whether something infringes on someone else's rights, or whether a license is the right legal choice for a given situation.
- It does not handle the separate question of what an author has agreed to let the hosting platform itself do with their work — that's a different, mandatory agreement the platform manages on its own.

## Why Some Licenses Get Retired Instead of Deleted

Occasionally a license offered in the past needs to be pulled from the list of new choices — because a newer version replaced it, legal counsel decided it shouldn't be offered anymore, or it was added by mistake. When that happens, Fithara keeps the retired license on file rather than deleting it, because a draft an author made earlier may still reference it. Retiring it just means it stops showing up as a choice for new drafts, while anything already using it keeps working.
